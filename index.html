What is AI & Innovation Club?

The AI & Innovation Club is a student community dedicated to exploring Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Prompt Engineering and emerging technologies.

The club provides students with opportunities to strengthen their knowledge and gain practical experience through projects, workshops, innovation challenges and collaborative activities.

Our goal is to encourage students to transform creative ideas into practical solutions while developing technical, problem-solving, teamwork and innovation skills.
